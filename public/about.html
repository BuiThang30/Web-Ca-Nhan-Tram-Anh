Introduction to THE VOCARBIT!

Choosing different subject combinations and deciding what career to pursue can feel overwhelming for students at any stage. Standardized personality tests, such as the MBTI test, exist to help with this life-changing problem. However, these tools are often unable to resonate with our actual lives - lack of context and specific sets of action. Because these scenarios are entirely unfamiliar, students often guess their answers and receive confusing career advice in return. That is what motivates THE VOCARBIT to be produced! The website consists of a clear explanation of its methodology, a set of 15 questions based on the original framework that has been adapted to include specific contexts. Above all, the results do not only include the code of personality, but it also decodes the outcome in terms of fields, tools and environment students should gravitate towards, as well as the subject combinations, set of skills and plan B that students should consider when adopting the recommended career path.

A few words from the publisher!!!!

I used to be a confused high school student who was torn between two contrasting subject combinations and chose the unsuitable one at last because of a lack of self-awareness. I used to be disheartened and sometimes even left behind because I could not simply fit in with the environment and the pace of my classmates, leading to anxiety and sleep deprivation. That stark reality was why I decided to build this website, not because I want to direct the younger generation to choose any specific fields, but I want them to experience their 12 years of academic pursuit without any disbelief in personal competency. Education, to me, is about learning with joy and growing up day by day, not discouragement and distress.
